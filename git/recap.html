Module 22 / 23 · about 20 minutes

Recall cards and a skill-building plan

Recall the right tool for the situation, review weak areas and preserve your browser-local learning progress.

By the end, you can…

  • Recall high-value commands and their boundaries.
  • Follow a practical progression from staging to team governance.
  • Back up or reset local learning data.

Retrieve before revealing

Start a five-minute recall round →

Say the command and its main risk before revealing the answer. “Know it” schedules the card for 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after successive successful reviews. “Again” returns it to immediate review. Due + new cards focuses the session; All cards is available any time.

Spend 20–30 minutes per practice session and revisit weak topics. Say what HEAD, the index, working tree and server reference will look like before each operation. Ask a teammate to explain a different solution and compare the tradeoffs.

Before integrating or rewriting

  • Confirm repository, branch, remote and target reference.
  • Inspect staged, unstaged and untracked work; preserve what matters.
  • Fetch and inspect divergence; identify the merge base and source commits.
  • Know who depends on the branch and which server rules apply.
  • Keep an appropriate committed recovery tip and separate backups for uncommitted files.
  • Choose merge, rebase, revert or a lease based on the actual intent.
  • Review the resulting graph, content and tests, then communicate the outcome.

Keep your learning progress

Lesson completion, lab observations, quiz answers, interactive exercise completion and recall schedules are stored on this browser when storage is available. Export before switching devices or clearing data. These records are learning aids, not proof that a real repository was inspected.

Remember in 30 seconds

  • Retrieve, predict, observe and explain.
  • Recovery, collaboration and permissions are skills to practise, not just read.
  • Back up browser-local progress if you want to retain it.
