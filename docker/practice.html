Practice lab

Reading is not knowing. Here are all the exercises of the course in one place: type the command, fill in the blank, or decide what to do in a real situation. Your answer is checked at once.

How the checking works

KindWhat you doHow it is checked
Type the commandWrite the full command and press EnterOptions may be in any order. -d -p, -dp and --detach --publish all count. If you are wrong, you are told which piece is missing.
Fill in the blankComplete a Dockerfile, a Compose file or a sentenceEach blank turns green or red on its own
ScenarioChoose what you would do in a real situationOne correct choice, with the reason
Best way to use it

Keep a terminal open next to this page. After the checker says "Correct", run the command for real and look at what Docker answers. Use Hint before Show answer.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The check runs in your browser, and your progress is saved in this browser only.
