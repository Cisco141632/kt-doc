How to get the most from it

  • Answer before you look anything up. A wrong answer that you then understand teaches more than a lucky guess.
  • Read the explanation even when you were right. It often says why, which is what interviews and real incidents ask for.
  • Missed a question? The review at the end links to the module that explains it.
  • Want to practise typing commands instead of choosing answers? Go to the practice lab.
