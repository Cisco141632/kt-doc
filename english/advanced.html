When the passive voice helps

Use it when the action or result matters more than the person doing it, or when the person is unknown.

TimePatternExample
usualam/is/are + past participleThe report is sent every Friday.

pastwas/were + past participleThe change was approved yesterday.
futurewill be + past participleThe update will be released tomorrow.
