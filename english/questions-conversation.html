Questions & conversation

Use a few reliable patterns to ask, answer, clarify, and keep the conversation moving.

Four question patterns

PatternUseExample
Do/Does + subject + verb?routines and factsDo you need help?
Did + subject + base verb?finished pastDid you complete the task?
Am/Is/Are + subject…?state or action nowAre you working on it?
Can/Could/Will + subject + verb?ability, request, futureCould you explain this?
Common correctionNot “Did you completed it?” After did, use the base verb: “Did you complete it?”

Say “not” correctly

PresentI do not understand.
She does not know.
PastI did not receive it.
We did not deploy.
NowI am not working today.
It is not responding.
FutureI will not be available.
We will not release it.

Use these when you do not understand

Could you repeat that, please?Ask to hear the same words again.
Could you say that more slowly?Ask the person to reduce speed.
What does “deadline” mean?Ask for the meaning of a word.
Do you mean that we should wait?Check your understanding.
