30 verbs you will use every day

PurposeWordsUseful sentence
Movementgo, come, leave, bring, takeI will bring it when I come.
Needsneed, want, get, give, useI need to get some water.
Thinkingknow, think, remember, forget, chooseI think I remember the name.
Communicationsay, tell, ask, call, explainPlease tell me what happened.
Actionmake, put, keep, find, tryI will try to find it.
Progressstart, finish, wait, help, payPlease wait while I finish this.

Daily sentence patterns

NeedI need + nounI need some water. / I need more time.
WantI would like + nounI would like a coffee, please.
LocationWhere is + thing?Where is the bus stop?
HelpCould you + verb?Could you help me, please?
TimeWhat time + question?What time will you arrive?
UnderstandingI do not understand + objectI do not understand this sentence.
