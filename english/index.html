Yes, this path is made for your condition

You can understand some English, but words do not always come back when you need them. You may know the idea but write only part of a sentence, make spelling mistakes, or feel confused by forms such as “is being fixed.” We will train those exact skills.

Your first confusing sentence

“It is being fixed” means “Someone is fixing it now.” You do not need to master the grammar today. You will practise it slowly in the advanced module.

How you will remember

1SeeConnect a picture to a useful word.
2HearListen and repeat the word or sentence.
3RecallHide the answer and bring it from memory.
4UseWrite and speak it in a real situation.

Recall ruleDo not only reread. First try without looking. A difficult recall creates a stronger memory than easy rereading.
