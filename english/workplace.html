Workplace English

Give useful updates, ask clear questions, and write messages that tell people what happens next.

Workplace picture vocabulary

Core workplace words

AreaWordsExample
Worktask, project, priority, deadline, progressThis task is my top priority.
Problemsissue, error, cause, blocker, solutionThe issue is blocking the release.
Peopleteam, manager, customer, owner, reviewerThe reviewer approved the change.
Actionscheck, update, review, send, sharePlease review and share your feedback.
Progressstart, complete, test, fix, deployWe tested the fix before deployment.
Planningdiscuss, decide, schedule, assign, confirmLet us schedule a short meeting.

The four-part work update

Status+problem+next action+time
Example“I completed the code change. One test is failing. I am fixing it now, and I will share another update by 3 PM.”
StartingI am starting the task now.
In progressI am working on the issue.
BlockedI am blocked because I do not have access.
FinishedI completed the task and updated the ticket.

Polite, clear requests

NeedSentence pattern
Ask for actionCould you please review this document?
Ask for meaningCould you clarify this requirement?
ConfirmCould you confirm the deadline?
Disagree carefullyI understand your point. I have a different suggestion.
Ask for timeCould we discuss this at 2 PM?
Follow upI am following up on my previous message.
Make it professional

Rewrite: “Review document.”

A message you can reuse

Status message template

Hi [name],

I am writing to share an update on [task]. I have completed [finished work]. I am currently working on [current work]. I expect to finish by [time].

Please let me know if you have any questions.

Thanks,
[your name]
