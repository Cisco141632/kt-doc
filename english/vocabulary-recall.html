Vocabulary recall

Knowing a word when you see it is recognition. Producing it without help is recall. We train recall.

The four-link memory

Picture↔Word↔Meaning↔Your sentence

Each new word needs all four links. If one link is weak, practise that link directly.

Mixed picture flashcards

  1. Look at the picture.
  2. Say the English word before revealing it.
  3. After revealing, read the example aloud.
  4. Rate honestly. “Again” is useful information, not failure.

A simple review system

AgainReview in 10 minutesYou could not recall it. See it, say it, cover it, recall it.
HardReview tomorrowYou recalled slowly or needed a hint.
GoodReview in three daysYou recalled correctly and used it in a sentence.
StrongReview next weekYou recalled it correctly on several days.

Add a word to your own memory

Paper or notes exercise

Choose one word you forgot today. Write:

  1. The word and its spelling.
  2. A very simple meaning.
  3. One true sentence about your life or work.
  4. One question using the word.

Example: deadline → the final time to finish → “My deadline is Friday.” → “Can we change the deadline?”
