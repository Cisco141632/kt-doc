Common corrections

Not thisUse thisWhy
I not understand.I do not understand.Present negative needs do not.
He work today.He works today.Add -s with he/she/it.
I am work now.I am working now.Use verb-ing after am for action now.
Did you completed?Did you complete it?Use base verb after did.
I have sent yesterday.I sent it yesterday.Specific finished time uses past simple.
Discuss about the issue.Discuss the issue.Discuss does not need about.
Explain me this.Explain this to me.Use explain something to someone.
Reply me.Reply to me.Reply needs to before a person.

Before you send a message

  1. Does every sentence have a subject and verb?
  2. Is the time clear: usual, now, past, or future?
  3. Did you say the problem and the next action?
  4. Did you include a time when it matters?
  5. Can you remove any unnecessary words?
  6. Did you check names, dates, and spelling?
