Your 20-minute daily practice

A short routine you can repeat every day is more powerful than a long lesson you rarely complete.

Write, compare, rewrite

Do this without the model first

Situation: You planned to finish a task today, but you found a problem. You need one more day.

  1. Write your message on paper or in your notes.
  2. Open the model and compare meaning, grammar, and clarity.
  3. Close the model and rewrite your improved message from memory.
Show model message

“Hi Sam, I found an issue while testing the change, so I need one more day to complete the task. I am fixing the issue now and expect to finish by tomorrow afternoon. I will share another update by 11 AM tomorrow.”

Measure real improvement

Once each week, write for five minutes without tools. Use the same prompt: “What did I do this week, what did I learn, and what will I do next?” Keep the writing. Compare it after four weeks for sentence length, clarity, vocabulary, and spelling.
