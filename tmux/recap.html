Recall, review & keep your progress

Refresh the high-value commands, reveal answers only after trying, and return to difficult cards later.

By the end, you can…

  • Recall a command from the task it performs.
  • Review weak cards instead of rereading everything.
  • Export your local learning progress.

Active recall cards

Read the task, say the key or command aloud, then reveal. “Know it” schedules this card for tomorrow, then in 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after successive successful reviews. “Again” resets it for immediate review. The review mode includes due and unseen cards; All cards lets you practise at any time.

Explain: why did my job survive closing the terminal but not a reboot?
The client connection ended when the terminal closed, but the server and pane process were still running. Reboot stopped those processes. Saved layouts do not save process memory.
Explain: why did copying into another tmux pane work but desktop paste fail?
The tmux paste buffer is separate from the desktop clipboard. The internal copy path worked; diagnose OSC 52 support or a local copy-pipe command and its display environment.
Explain: why did my script type the right command but produce the wrong result?
Input goes to the foreground program in the target pane. The pane may be busy, at another prompt or incorrectly targeted. send-keys success is not application success.

Your progress belongs to this browser

Completed lessons, lab checkboxes, quiz answers, browser mission completions and recall schedules are saved locally when storage is available. They are learning aids, not evidence of verified terminal work. Export before switching browsers or clearing site data.

Remember in 30 seconds

  • Retrieve first; reveal second.
  • Use Again for uncertain cards and revisit the related real lab.
  • Back up browser-local progress before clearing it.
